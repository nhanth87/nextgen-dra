**Finding — DA-PA1-001** (accepted): In `DiameterWireCodec.decode()`, the untrusted AVP header `avp-length` field is validated only against the 8-byte base AVP header. When the V (vendor) bit is set, the header is actually 12 bytes, yet the decoder still reads a 4-byte Vendor-Id (`readUnsignedInt`) and derives a negative data length. A crafted vendor AVP with `avp-length` in [8..11] produces a +4-byte buffer over-read per AVP (accumulating across chained vendor AVPs), which can read past the declared Diameter message length and out of the underlying `ByteBuf`.
